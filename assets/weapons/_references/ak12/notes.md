# AK-12 (6P70) - reference notes

Asset id: `weapon_ak12`. Builder: `tools/blender/weapons/` (see the module docstring).

## Variant

2020 production: railed dust cover with the rear sight at its back, railed handguard, telescopic side-folding stock.

## Dimensions

| Quantity | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Overall length | 880-940 mm (stock range) / 690 mm folded | published (Kalashnikov / Rosoboronexport via Wikipedia) |
| Barrel | 415 mm | published |
| Calibre | 5.45x39 mm | published |

"Published" = taken from the cited public specification; "estimated" = measured on the reference photographs or chosen to fit them.

## Recognisable features modelled

Ergonomic grip, ambidextrous-style selector, new muzzle device, magazine with round-count windows.
