# HK417 (Norwegian designated marksman rifle) - reference notes

Asset id: `weapon_hk417`. Builder: `tools/blender/weapons/` (see the module docstring).

## Variant

HK417 16" with Schmidt & Bender PM II-class scope and folded bipod.

## Dimensions

| Quantity | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Overall length | 1.03 m used (stock extended) | approximate - published HK417 16" figures vary with the stock position; the model is checked against 1.03 m |
| Barrel | 406 mm (16") | published (Wikipedia) |
| Calibre | 7.62x51 mm NATO | published |

"Published" = taken from the cited public specification; "estimated" = measured on the reference photographs or chosen to fit them.

## Recognisable features modelled

Larger receiver than the HK416, 20-round steel/polymer magazine, quad rail, scope with large objective.
