# HK416 (Norwegian HK416N) - reference notes

Asset id: `weapon_hk416`. Builder: `tools/blender/weapons/` (see the module docstring).

## Variant

HK416N with 14.5" barrel as issued to the Norwegian Army; Aimpoint CompM4-class red dot, vertical grip, HK steel 30-round magazine.

## Dimensions

| Quantity | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Overall length | ≈0.98 m (stock extended) | estimated - traced from the HK416N reference photo, 0.78 mm/px |
| Barrel | 368 mm (14.5") | published (HK / Wikipedia) |
| Calibre | 5.56x45 mm NATO | published |

"Published" = taken from the cited public specification; "estimated" = measured on the reference photographs or chosen to fit them.

## Recognisable features modelled

Free-floating quad rail, HK slim-line stock, folding iron sights, ambidextrous selector, HK flash hider, steel magazine.
