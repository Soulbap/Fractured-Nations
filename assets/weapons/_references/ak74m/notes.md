# AK-74M - reference notes

Asset id: `weapon_ak74m`. Builder: `tools/blender/weapons/` (see the module docstring).

## Variant

Black polymer furniture, side-folding solid stock, plum 5.45 magazine.

## Dimensions

| Quantity | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Overall length | 943 mm / 700 mm folded | published (Wikipedia / Kalashnikov) |
| Barrel | 415 mm | published |
| Grip→muzzle / grip→butt | 0.657 m / 0.286 m | estimated - traced from the Kalashnikov Commons photo, 1.50 mm/px |
| Calibre | 5.45x39 mm | published |

"Published" = taken from the cited public specification; "estimated" = measured on the reference photographs or chosen to fit them.

## Recognisable features modelled

Stamped receiver, ribbed dust cover, side scope rail, tangent rear sight, two-chamber muzzle brake, ribbed lower handguard.
