# VEH-05 Leopard 2A8 NOR — reference notes

**Variant modelled:** Leopard 2 A8 NOR (Norway ordered 54 in 2023; KNDS / Ritek Levanger; first two delivered to Rena on
30 April 2026): 2A8 hull and turret with arrowhead wedge armour, 120 mm Rh-120 L55A1, EuroTrophy (Trophy HV) active protection,
Norwegian lightweight side skirts with containers for winter grousers, Kongsberg ICS / CORTEX (internal, not visible).

## Recognition features modelled
Long flat hull with glacis add-on armour module, driver's hatch and periscopes front left, front fenders with headlight guards,
six-panel skirts per side (heavier front panel), winter grouser containers on the rear fenders, engine deck with grilles,
exhaust louvres in the rear plate; arrowhead turret with vertical sides, side stowage boxes, bustle basket, gunner's main sight
(front right), commander's PERI on a pedestal (rear right), commander and loader hatches, crosswind sensor, smoke dischargers;
EuroTrophy launchers on the turret sides with radar panels at the front and rear corners; 120 mm L55A1 with thermal sleeve,
fume extractor and muzzle reference sensor.

## Uncertainties
The remote weapon station of the Norwegian tanks is not confirmed in the sources found (the German A8 carries an FLW 200): a
Protector-type RWS on the loader's side stands in and is marked as an estimate. The first NOR tanks were shown without user
camouflage; the asset uses NATO three-colour as on Norwegian Leopard 2A4NO. Radar panel size and position are estimates.
