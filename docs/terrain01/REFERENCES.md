## Scots pine (*Pinus sylvestris*), Sweden

Observed: tall straight stems with the crown only in the top 25–35 % in stands, flat/domed
umbrella crowns in old trees, grey-brown plated bark low on the stem turning thin orange-red
higher up, clumped needle masses on spreading branches, moss and blueberry floor, mossy erratics.

## Norway spruce (*Picea abies*), Sweden / Norway

Observed: narrow conical silhouette, dense dark crown reaching low in open-grown and edge trees,
dead grey twigs on the lower stem inside stands, drooping lower branches, dark grey-brown bark,
dark moss floor with little light.

## Birch (*Betula pubescens*, *B. pendula*), Norway

Observed: slender stems, white bark with black horizontal lenticels and a dark rough base,
light, airy crowns of fine drooping twigs, often in groups and on wet ground or forest edges,
low mountain birch near the treeline.

- Commons media search "birch forest Norway bjørkeskog" — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=birch+forest+Norway+bj%C3%B8rkeskog&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image

## Boulders, outcrops (granite / gneiss), Finland

Observed: rounded-angular grey blocks half buried, pale grey-green crustose lichen patches,
moss caps on top surfaces, scattered through open pine forest.

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rocky_pine_forest_near_Karhunpesävaara_and_Myössäjärvi_in_Inari,_Lapland,_Finland,_2021_September.jpg

## Rural buildings, Norway

Observed: Falu-red timber barns with white window frames and corner boards, steep gable roofs
(grey/black), ramps to the upper barn floor, white or red farmhouses, small sheds.
