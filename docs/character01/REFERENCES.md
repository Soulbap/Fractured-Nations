## Major design decisions

* **Uniform**: one loose two-piece combat uniform in the M23 cut (jacket over trousers,
  integrated knee protectors, pockets as in the Forsvaret photos) instead of a combat shirt
  under armour; it reads correctly with and without a vest. The camouflage is an original
  pattern with the M23's structure (mid-green field, large khaki shapes, dark-green clusters
  with dotted edges, sparse near-black dots) at a 0.6 m tile.
* **Helmet**: high-cut FAST/Caiman class shell (above the ears for the headset), side rails,
  NVG shroud, harness with chin cup. Standard loadout has a camouflage cover (as in most
  Norwegian photos); the heavy loadout shows the bare ranger-green shell with NVG and
  counterweight. Shell 21 x 26 cm outer, so the head-to-helmet ratio stays realistic.
* **Carrier colours**: ranger green for the standard soldier (Norwegian issue look), coyote
  for the heavy load (mixed coyote/green kit is common in the photos and separates the two
  silhouettes at RTS distance).
* **Weapon**: an original carbine with HK416N proportions rather than a copy; the stock is
  collapsed two positions as soldiers in armour do, which also keeps the support hand within
  reach in the low-ready carry.
* **Carry**: low ready (stock in the shoulder pocket, muzzle ~30 deg down, slight blading
  with the support shoulder forward), the dominant carry in the patrol photos; sprint uses
  a port-arms carry across the chest.
* **Face**: generic adult male, beard shadow and short hair; eye protection and the headset
  are worn in both loadouts as in the photos, which also keeps the face simple.
